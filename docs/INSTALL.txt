SPLIT LEDGER — Android app (split_expense.apk)
==============================================

App name on the phone : Split Ledger
Package               : com.sherinlal.splitledger
Version               : 2.5 (versionCode 9)
Size                  : 61 KB
Works on              : Android 6.0 (Marshmallow, 2015) and newer
Permissions           : internet only — no contacts, no storage, no location


HOW TO INSTALL
--------------
1. Copy split_expense.apk to your phone (USB cable, Google Drive, or email it
   to yourself and download it on the phone).

2. On the phone, open Files and tap split_expense.apk.

3. Android will say installs from this source are blocked. Tap Settings in
   that message, turn on "Allow from this source" for whichever app you are
   installing from (Files / Chrome / Drive), then press back and tap Install.
   This is normal for any app that did not come from the Play Store.

4. If Play Protect shows "Unsafe app blocked" or "App scan", choose
   "Install anyway" / "More details > Install anyway". Play Protect flags every
   app it has not seen before; it is not reporting anything it found.

5. Open "Split Ledger" from your app drawer.

Install 2.0 straight over any earlier version — same signing key, so your
groups and expenses are kept.


WHAT'S NEW IN 2.2
-----------------
* Fixed a bug that silently lost changes. If you saved an expense while the
  app happened to be mid-way through talking to the server, that expense was
  dropped from the queue and never sent — the app showed "Synced" and the
  other person never saw it. Anything queued while a request is in flight is
  now kept and sent on the next round.

  If you have been running 2.1, open each group once after updating and check
  that everything you expect is there. An expense lost this way is still on
  the phone that created it; editing it (change the amount and change it back)
  puts it back in the queue.


WHAT WAS NEW IN 2.1
-----------------
* Your mobile number is now your account. The first screen asks for your
  number and your name. No OTP, no password — see the warning below.

* Groups are shared. Add someone to a group by their mobile number and, when
  they sign in on their own phone with that number, the group, its members and
  every expense in it appear for them. Both of you can add expenses and each
  sees the other's.
  This needs a server. Until you set one up (→ Account, in the ☰ drawer) the
  app works exactly as before, on this phone only.

* Everyone sees their own names. A member is identified by their NUMBER, and
  the name shown is whatever YOU have that number saved as. If you save a
  number as "Anu" and your friend saves it as "Anu Sharma", each of you sees
  your own name for her. Use "Rename" next to a member to pick a different
  name from your contacts — it only changes what you see.

* Works with no signal. Add expenses on a train; they queue on the phone and
  sync when the connection comes back. The drawer shows Synced / Offline.


WHO CAN SEE YOUR GROUPS — PLEASE READ
-------------------------------------
There is no verification of mobile numbers. Anyone who types your number into
their own copy of the app, pointed at your server, becomes you and sees your
groups.

That is a deliberate trade for a private server used by people who know each
other. Do not hand the server address to strangers. The code to switch on SMS
verification is already written — set REQUIRE_OTP=true on the server and add
an SMS provider — see backend/README.md.


WHAT WAS NEW IN 2.0
-----------------
* Passcode lock. The first launch asks you to set a four-digit passcode, and
  it is asked for every time you open the app and again after two minutes in
  the background. Five wrong tries pause entry for 30 seconds.
  Manage it from the lock icon at the bottom of the ☰ drawer: lock now,
  change it, or turn it off.

* Add members from your contacts. In Group settings there is now a Contacts
  button next to Add. It opens your phone's own contact picker; tap a person
  and their name and number are added to the group.
  The app does NOT read your address book and asks for no contacts
  permission — the picker runs outside the app and hands back only the one
  person you tapped.

* Fixed the "This app was built for an older version of Android" message.
  The app now declares Android 6.0 as its minimum and Android 14 as its
  target, so the installer stops treating it as a legacy app.


ABOUT THE PASSCODE — WHAT IT DOES AND DOESN'T DO
------------------------------------------------
The passcode is never stored. What is stored is a salted, 25,000-round
SHA-256 of it, so the number itself cannot be read back off the phone.

It stops someone who picks up your unlocked phone from opening the app and
reading your groups. It is NOT encryption: the expense data itself is stored
in the app's private storage in the clear, so it does not protect against
someone with full access to the phone's filesystem. Your phone's own screen
lock is what does that.

There is no way to recover a forgotten passcode. The only way back in is
"Forgot your passcode?" on the lock screen, which erases the app's data.
Keep a backup (see below) if the data matters.


IMPORTANT — YOUR DATA LIVES ON THE PHONE
----------------------------------------
The app has no internet access and no account. Everything is stored in the
app's own storage on that one device. That means:

  * It does NOT sync with the web version, and two phones do not share data.
  * Uninstalling the app deletes everything in it.
  * Use the Backup button (open the ☰ drawer, then the download icon at the
    bottom) to copy your data out as text. Paste that same text into the
    Restore box on another phone, or after reinstalling, to get it all back.


WHAT'S IN IT
------------
Everything the web version has: groups, members, expenses with all five split
types (equal / exact / percentage / shares / adjustments), single or multiple
payers, balances, simplified settle-up, recorded payments, insights charts and
an activity feed.

It opens on a sample group called "Goa Trip 2026" so you can see it working.
Delete it from the group settings (gear icon) once you have made a real group.


UPDATING IT LATER
-----------------
split-ledger.jks is the key this APK was signed with. Keep it. Android only
lets a new version install over an old one if it is signed with the SAME key —
without this file a future version would have to be installed as a separate
app, losing the data.

  keystore : split-ledger.jks
  alias    : splitledger
  password : splitledger   (both the store and the key)

split_expense_source.zip has everything used to build the APK.
